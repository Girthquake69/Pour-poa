Kenyan Sour

House Signature. Silky · Citrus · Classic

Hibiscus Sunset

Floral · Tropical · Bright

Vodka brightened with pineapple and lemon, lifted by ruby hibiscus syrup, a vibrant, sun kissed pour.

Blue Lagoon

Crisp · Citrus · Playful

Vodka and triple sec shaken with lemon, topped with Sprite and blue curaçao, cool, sparkling, and bold in colour.

Mango Chilli

Sweet · Spicy · Tropical

Vodka with ripe mango juice, lime, and syrup, a lively play of heat and sweetness.

Pineapple Rum Punch

Fruity · Bold · Festive

Long Island Iced Coffee

Strong · Coffee · Indulgent

Vodka, rum, tequila, and whiskey meet coffee liqueur, fresh coffee, and cream liqueur, a rich, dessert style classic.

Moscow Mule

Ginger · Zesty · Refreshing

Spirit with KC lemon and ginger, sharpened by lime and lengthened with ginger ale, spicy, clean, and sharp.

Vanilla Coffee

Creamy · Smooth · Comforting

Vanilla vodka, cream liqueur, coffee, and milk, sweetened with syrup, soft, rich, and aromatic.

Classic Mimosa

Bright · Bubbly · Simple

Fresh squeezed orange juice and sparkling wine, with ratios set for optimal balance.

Passion Spritz

Tropical · Light · Festive

Aperol or a house bitter with fresh passion fruit, prosecco, soda, and mint.

Peach Bellini

Soft · Fruity · Elegant

House peach purée and sparkling wine, clean, soft, and celebratory.

Grapefruit Paloma Spritz

Zesty · Refreshing · Modern

Sunrise Cooler
